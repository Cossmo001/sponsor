The Concept

Our host steps out of their comfort zone to live and work exactly as everyday Nigerians do for 1 to 3 days. Whether braving the sun as a traffic hawker, navigating the chaotic energy of the local markets as a trader, or experiencing the grueling hours of a daily wage worker, this series captures the unfiltered reality of making a living on the streets of Nigeria.
